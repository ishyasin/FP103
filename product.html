FP10 Manager™

Covers the two distinct types of FP10 controlled stationery in circulation across the Trust, replacing manual serial-range logging with an accurate, auditable digital record.

FP10HNC Manager — tracks handwritten pads booked out to a department, generating the pharmacy register, cover sheets and session log needed for a full audit trail.
FP10SS Manager — automatically calculates the exact number of FP10SS sheets in a stack by entering just four serial numbers. It generates a ready-to-print pharmacy/ward audit log, eliminating the need to manually count sheets from a box of 2,000. This saves time, prevents counting errors and reduces the risk of damaging forms during handling.

PRISM™

Patient Risk Identification System & Medicines Reconciliation

A GD&G Special Projects™ tool. PRISM is a self-contained, zero-server web application that automates the most time-consuming part of Emergency Department medicines reconciliation: turning a patient's Summary Care Record into a structured, ready-to-use clinical document.

How it works — Pharmacists or pharmacy technicians drop one or more GP Summary Care Record exports straight from NCRS onto the screen. PRISM parses each record automatically, cross-references every medicine and condition listed against a built-in, DM+D/SNOMED-aligned reference list and applies a three-tier risk stratification (Red/Amber/Green) to flag anticoagulants, antiepileptics and other high-risk medicines.

Each patient is presented as an at-a-glance tile showing their risk band, flagged medications and significant co-morbidities, so the pharmacy team can triage its highest-risk patients first. Tiles can be dragged into an "Under Review" queue.

Automated Medicines Reconciliation Transcription — The core time-saver is PRISM's automatic Med Rec document generation. For any selected patient, PRISM transcribes the parsed SCR data directly into a standard Medicines Reconciliation format, with one-click A–Z sorting of repeat medication lists. The finished document exports as clipboard-ready text or an RTF file for direct use in EPR.

The Bee List™

Bee List is a digital ward stock management platform designed to streamline stock ordering and governance.

Live Ward Stock Visibility — Instantly view the current stock held on individual wards.
Ad Hoc Stock Ordering — Submit ward stock requests quickly and efficiently through a centralised system.
Comprehensive Audit Trail — Maintain a complete record of all stock requests, approvals and ordering activity for governance and reporting.
Secure User Accounts — Individual usernames and passwords for nursing and pharmacy staff provide accountability and full user traceability.
Real-Time Communication — View notes relating to outstanding requests, follow-up actions, stock shortages and supply issues directly within the system.
Order Transparency — Ward staff can see items ordered within the previous 48 hours, reducing unnecessary repeat requests.
Intelligent Duplicate Detection — Automatic alerts notify users when an item has already been ordered, helping to prevent duplicate orders and reduce waste.

Bee List simplifies ward stock management by improving communication, increasing transparency and reducing duplication.
