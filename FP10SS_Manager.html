About this tool

FP10 Manager

Developed by GD&G Special Projects™ for University Hospitals Sussex NHS Foundation Trust, FP10 Manager™ covers the two distinct types of FP10 controlled stationery in circulation across the Trust.

FP10HNC Manager is for the handwritten pads — FP10HNC forms, typically issued in pads of 50, filled in by hand at the point of prescribing. As pads are booked out to a department, it generates the pharmacy register, cover sheets, and session log needed to track that pad through to return — a full audit trail without anyone re-keying serial ranges by hand.

FP10SS Manager is for the machine-printed single sheets — FP10SS forms, overprinted via Symphony. Quantities in this volume can't practically be counted sheet by sheet, so this tool works out exactly how many sheets are in a stack from just the first three serials and the last one, then produces a Pharmacy Copy / Ward Copy log ready to print.

This tool is intended for use by authorised Trust staff only.

How to Use

1. Take the serial numbers from the first three sheets at the top of the stack and enter them in order.

2. Turn to the last sheet at the bottom of the stack and enter its serial number, then enter the department / FP10 code — required for the log. The name attached to the log is taken automatically from your account.

3. Click Count Sheets & Generate Log. The tool detects the pad's numbering pattern from your first three entries, works forward to the last serial to tell you exactly how many sheets are in the stack, and prepares the log entry for this session.

4. Click Download Log (PDF) in the Pharmacy Log panel. The PDF contains a Pharmacy Copy and a Ward Copy of the same log, ready to print and split between the two.

Each session produces one log entry. To log a different stack, clear the log first and count again.

If the fields turn red, double-check the numbers — either they weren't entered in order, or the last serial doesn't belong to the same batch/pad as the first three.
