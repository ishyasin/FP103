About this tool

FP10 Manager

Developed by GD&G Special Projects™ for University Hospitals Sussex NHS Foundation Trust, FP10 Manager™ covers the two distinct types of FP10 controlled stationery in circulation across the Trust.

FP10HNC Manager is for the handwritten pads — FP10HNC forms, typically issued in pads of 50, filled in by hand at the point of prescribing. As pads are booked out to a department, it generates the pharmacy register, cover sheets, and session log needed to track that pad through to return — a full audit trail without anyone re-keying serial ranges by hand.

FP10SS Manager is for the machine-printed single sheets — FP10SS forms, overprinted via Symphony. Quantities in this volume can't practically be counted sheet by sheet, so this tool works out exactly how many sheets are in a stack from just the first three serials and the last one, then produces a Pharmacy Copy / Ward Copy log ready to print.

This tool is intended for use by authorised Trust staff only.

Step-by-step guide

How to Use FP10 Manager

Each register corresponds to a single physical FP10 pad. Always have the pad in front of you when entering serial numbers.
1
Enter your name
When the app loads you will be prompted for your name. This is recorded on all generated documents. You can also update it in the session bar at the top of the page.
2
Add a register
Click + Add Register in the navigation bar. Enter the department FP10 / RYR/UHS code (e.g. RYR123), the number of prescriptions per pad (typically 50), and the first three 11-digit serial numbers from the front of the pad. Fields turn green when the pattern is valid.
3
Add more pads (if needed)
After saving a register the serial fields clear automatically. The department code and pad count are kept so you can enter the next pad's serials straight away. Click Close when you have finished adding pads for this session.
4
End the session & download PDFs
Click End Session. You will see a download button for each register (cover sheet + full tracking register with one row per prescription) and a pharmacy session log summarising all pads issued in this session.
ⓘ
Session restore
If you close the browser tab accidentally your session is saved for up to 24 hours. Reopen the app and choose Restore when prompted.
